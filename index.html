Topic:

Investigation on Career Opportunities in the Gig Economy

Focus Question: How does the gig economy creates technology focused jobs?


Abstract

This investigation explores how the gig economy impacts technology-focused career opportunities, with a particular emphasis on freelance programming within South Africa. The study examines the role of digital platforms—such as Upwork, Fiverr, and LinkedIn—in connecting skilled freelancers with global and local businesses requiring specialized technical services. It further evaluates the feasibility of freelance programming as a sustainable career path for young South Africans, weighing benefits like schedule flexibility and skill development against challenges such as income instability for beginners. The findings highlight the critical importance of digital literacy, project management, and continuous upskilling using tools such as Visual Studio Code, GitHub, and learning platforms. Ultimately, while the gig economy expands access to technology-driven jobs, long-term success depends on technical proficiency, effective rate management, and strong business communication.

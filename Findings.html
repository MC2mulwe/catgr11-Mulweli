How to start looking for jobs on Upwork.

To start freelancing on Upwork, you need to do the following:

  1. Sign up for an account by clicking on the sign up button on the Upwork's website
  2. Choose the option to join as a freelancer
  3. Create an account, either using a Google account; Apple account or an email address.
  4. Add your first and last name as well as a strong password and location details
  5. PLEASE ACTUALLY READ THE TERMS OF SERVICE; USER AGREEMENT AND PRIVACY POLICY(Or dont...)
  6. Answer the questions that Upwork gives you to help set up your profile
  7. Import in your resume or/and LinkedIn profile
  8. Customise your profile by choosing your tags to discribe what you do
  9. Add work experience; languages you speak and your education
  10. Write a bio and job title (make sure it is highly specific and specialised)
    i.e. - “Email Marketing Specialist | HubSpot Certified” is better than “Digital Marketer”
  11. Set your rates
  12. Take a profile photo and upload it
...And voila! You now have a Upwork account and can start looking for jobs.

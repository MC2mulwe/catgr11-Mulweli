What is the Gig-Economy?

The Gig-Economy is a service-based market that emphasizes shorter-term or freelance work, which is different than traditional employment where you work for one client.

Links to Resources: Investopedia Team, Margaret James and Katrina Munichiello. (2026) Understanding the gig economy: flexible jobs explained.



What skills are needed to work in freelance programming?

Freelancers, in general, need to have the following skills:

  1. Good Marketing of their Skills
  2. Negotiating Contracts with Clients
  3. Good Communication Skills
  4. Time-Management Skills
  5. Digital Literacy
  6. Industry Knowledge
  7. Project Management
As well as skills in programming dependant on what you want do and the language you choose to program in.

Links to Resources: TED-Ed (2022) What is a gig economy? (Accessed: June 30, 2026)



Why is digital literacy important for gig economy careers?

Digital Literacy allows for gig workers to do some of the gig work they do as well as helping gig workers to:

  • Gain Extra Income
  • Help Upskill themselves through learning new technologies and skills
  • Do Research
  • Participate in collaborative projects
  • Help communicate with customers
  • Practice online safety
Having digital literacy can also help gig workers gain more permanent job opportunities, if the gig worker wants to do so. Digital literacy also helps programming freelancers identify errors in their code.
